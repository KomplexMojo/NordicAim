Shot detection review

How to review. Each photo shows exactly what the app's detector found, drawn on the same 1200 px image it analysed.
  • Every detection is ONE hole. The #1, #2… beside a circle is only its rank (most confident first), never a shot count.
  • Each photo has a mode switch. In Mark detections, tap a green circle that is not a real hole to mark it wrong (it turns red; tap again to undo), or tap an orange ✕ that actually was a hole.
  • In Add missed hole, every tap adds a pink marker exactly where you tap — even right beside a green circle. Tap a pink marker to remove it.
  • Carry over earlier marks reapplies your previous review where the positions still match: wrong-detection marks land on a detection within a hole's width, and holes you marked before that are not detected now come back as pink markers.
  • Then rate the rings and the detection and add a comment. Only the target the app analysed counts. Ignore holes in the backing board and any second target in the photo. Ratings save in this page as you go. Use Export ratings to send them back.
